# Product walkthrough

1. Open https://crosscampus-kr.web.app and keep **체험 공간** selected.
2. As **Jin**, open **바다의 목소리를, 5분의 영화로.** and apply for the design role with a first-week proposal.
3. Switch **체험 역할** to **Mina**. Open **모집 관리** and accept Jin.
4. Open **우리 팀 · 첫 주**, set the first goal and meeting, and complete a checklist item.
5. Switch back to **Jin**. The same team board and completed task are visible.
6. Below the checklist, under **첫 결과물**, press **결과물 남기기** and add a title, an `https://` link, your contribution and a short reflection.
7. Switch to **Mina**. Leave feedback on Jin's result. Mina cannot edit or delete it.
8. Switch to **Jin** and edit or delete the result. Deleting asks for confirmation and removes its feedback too. As **Yuri** (still pending), the team board and results are hidden.

All participants, school names, and projects in this walkthrough are fictional. State is stored in the browser. **체험 처음부터** resets only this demo.

Real projects use Google sign-in, an invitation list, and Firestore persistence. The public demo is suitable for evaluating the product flow; it is not evidence of student adoption.

Recording a result and peer feedback is described in [issue #1](https://github.com/eonia89/crosscampus/issues/1) and [the feature spec](features/sprint-outcomes.md).
